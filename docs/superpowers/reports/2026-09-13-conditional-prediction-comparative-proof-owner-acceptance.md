## 4. Accepted conclusion and limitations

Existing application composition suffices for the tested bounded case.
Accountable claim representation did not demonstrate a necessary backend
extension. This establishes neither empirical predictive validity nor sufficiency
for every future application. It does not establish that conditional predictions
are unnecessary or that a backend extension could never be justified.

BRC's conditional-prediction gap remains: the tested existing contracts did not
derive those predictions. Packaging supplied premises does not resolve their
evidential justification. Explicit authority and citations make a claim
accountable; they do not establish prediction accuracy, causality or real faults.

Objective 3 receives evidence about comparing supplied explanations, not actual
diagnosis. Objective 4 receives bounded candidate-set selection evidence, not
globally optimal or operationally safe next-test delivery. Objective 7 receives
provenance and epistemic-classification evidence. Objective 5 change impact and
objective 6 planning remain open. No production capability was added.

Remaining questions concern prediction origin and empirical validation,
applicability outside this population, hypothesis completeness, and whether a
future concrete shared lifecycle, model fact or integrity boundary requires
backend ownership. Such a requirement must be demonstrated rather than inferred
from hypothetical reuse. No next investigation or successor is selected here.
