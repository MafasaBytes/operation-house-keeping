## 6. Accepted conclusion, limits and architectural disposition

Existing application composition performed the tested named-decision dependency
review and equation-based one-step projection under explicit synthetic effect,
uncertainty, applicability and policy assumptions. This is useful computation over
supplied premises: it derives consequences, reevaluation sets, target relations,
finite-set feasibility and tied least-cost selections rather than merely restating
a dependency edge or a supplied outcome matrix.

Acceptance establishes implementation and mathematical consistency for this finite
synthetic world. It does not establish backend necessity, physical accuracy, causal
identification, hypothesis completeness, action generation, multi-step planning,
global impact or global minimality. No command was issued, no physical action or
observation occurred, and no empirical holdout was evaluated.

Objectives 3–7 receive bounded evidence, not closure. Objective 3 still lacks real
diagnosis and causal/empirical adequacy. Objective 4 still lacks candidate-test
generation and a validated utility/safety policy. Objective 5 still lacks global
impact and physically validated propagation. Objective 6 still lacks action
generation, multi-step reachability and global planning. Objective 7 retains the
tested distinction among supplied assumptions, public facts, calculations,
observations, unknowns, inapplicability and refusals, without authenticating truth.
No original objective is marked fully achieved.

IN-001 remains open and unselected. A directed influence declaration might support
shared dependency discovery, but this proof did not test it and a declaration alone
would not supply effect equations, timing, uncertainty, applicability, invariance
or causal truth. CI-001 remains open and unselected. The bounded application review
answered which named decisions require reevaluation; it did not test backend-owned
completeness, lifecycle, replay or generalized impact semantics. Neither option is
authorized or disproved.

Outstanding architecture questions are therefore evidence-gated rather than

answer under equal premises, validators, oracle effort and limits. A future proposal
must name that question and ownership boundary. This closeout authorizes none.
