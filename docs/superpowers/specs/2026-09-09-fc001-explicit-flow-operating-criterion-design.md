## 4. Immutable subject, body and witness semantics

The immutable correction subject S is exactly the accepted FM subject:

```text
(flow_anchor_id, variable_anchor_id, participant_port_anchor_id,
 exact AssertionContext, fixed direct_measurement_relevance, orientation)
```

The fixed relevance dimension is represented by FMA's accepted kind and measured-actual VSM support, not a new stored token. Context is the full tagged `AssertionContext`, with no lifecycle-view fallback. Orientation is exactly `aligned`, `opposite`, `unsigned_magnitude`, or `not_applicable`.

The governed FM body B is S plus these exact record IDs, in this semantic tuple order:

```text
(flow_registration_record_id, variable_registration_record_id,
 port_registration_record_id, flow_declaration_record_id,
 variable_port_association_record_id, variable_semantic_declaration_record_id)
```

Use the accepted canonical FM projection in [canonical.py](../../../backend/model_kernel/canonical.py). It includes the five serialized subject dimensions and these six pins. It excludes record ID, authority, evidence, recorded time, note and supersedes. The fixed relevance dimension is implicit, not an extra key in that accepted projection.

An FC positive record designates an exact CRG/VCN for B. Its effective-body equality key is **(B, exact CRG ID, exact VCN ID)**. Its record identity also includes the exact nominated FMA witness and all ordinary record provenance. Thus two FC records naming different same-body witnesses can be different records but equal governing bodies. Body equality is not predicate equivalence, shared authority, evidence equivalence or a new identity.

The witness remains a mandatory exact member and historical provenance reference. Withdrawing or superseding it does not delete it and does not by itself revoke FC. Another healthy active FMA assertion with identical B can supply current endorsement. Omitting the witness makes FC closure invalid even if an equal-body assertion remains. Never choose a witness automatically, rewrite its stored reference, follow its lineage to a changed B, or store an opaque body digest instead of reconstructible support.

Changing S requires a new parentless FC root. Within S, a correction can explicitly change witness, B's support editions, CRG/VCN, evidence or provenance. CRG must always target S's variable. Exact VCN renewal is mandatory after any supersession, even an apparently identical predicate. Existing ConstraintAnchor, FlowAnchor, VariableAnchor, PortAnchor and FMA identities are unchanged.

## 9. Exact lifecycle and historical replay

A parentless FCD starts one independent root identified derivationally by that record ID. Every correction parent is a membership-present positive FCD of the same S and same derived root. Root IDs are indexes/provenance, not durable anchors or authority priorities. A changed CRG/VCN or support edition is a new explicit body within S, not an automatic retargeting.

Successor relations are derived from all parent edges. Current positive tips have no successor and no FCW target. Zero tips means inactive; one means healthy; two or more means conflicted, even with equal bodies. A correction over every current same-root fork tip can converge the root. A partial correction leaves uncited sibling branches alive. No rule may infer convergence merely from equal bodies, timestamps or record order.

FCW targets one retained positive FCD with no successor and no other withdrawal in the selected membership. Selective withdrawal of one fork tip is legal. Unknown, wrong-kind, superseded, duplicate-withdrawn targets reject. Withdrawal is never a parent; a withdrawn target cannot have a successor in a valid membership, regardless of alleged append times. A new assertion after complete withdrawal starts a new root.

Independent healthy roots with equal FC bodies corroborate. Different active bodies disagree. Inactive roots retain all provenance but do not veto active agreement. “Different criteria” is selection disagreement for one slot, not a claim that legitimate engineering requirements contradict one another. Do not model a checklist by inventing contexts/orientations or treating different roots as independent mandatory slots.

Replay selects exact record membership, not records with `recorded_at <= cutoff`. It must retain parents, witness, exact support records and all required model-evidence closure. Omitting a withdrawal can revive its former target; omitting a required witness/parent cannot be repaired by an equal body elsewhere. Replay re-derives all indexes and revision IDs. Historical selected knowledge is not effective-time or physical history. Existing accepted evidence-closure rules are unchanged: model evidence cycles, if membership-valid, are not recursively treated as proof; use visited closure and the graph budgets.

## 10. Present-applicability algorithm and decision table

For S, enumerate **all** FC and FM roots from the selected full membership. Root health is local tip cardinality. Cross-root disagreement means there exist current tips from two distinct roots with different effective bodies; tips in one fork alone produce same-root conflict, not cross-root disagreement. Both findings can coexist.

Choose a governing FC body group only if at least one FC tip exists, every active FC root is healthy, and all active FC tips have equal FC body. Inactive roots do not participate. This is equality-based agreement, not a selected representative. The designated group can be inapplicable due to lower support; retain its index in that case.

For every historical FC body group, independently derive its lower eligibility reasons:

- FM must have at least one active assertion for S. All active FM roots must be healthy, all their tips must agree on B, and at least one active tip must have this group's B. Same-body fork tips still conflict; unrelated active B2 creates cross-root disagreement. Retained withdrawn witness activity alone does not gate B.
- FRG/VRG/PRG/CRG are permanent member registrations; no fictitious “latest registration” exists.
- Exact direct FLD/VPA/VSM pins must each be current, healthy and aligned under accepted FM disclosure. Alignment `exact` alone does not imply current or healthy.
- Exact selected FLD PCN plus **every** selected source/sink PIF direction pin must be current, healthy and have FL alignment `exact_current`. These are selected-model eligibility gates. Do not add global FL-root agreement, unrelated PIF dimensions, PCM compatibility or physical-connectivity assumptions.
- The exact VCN must be the sole current declaration of its ConstraintAnchor. Conflict and a superseded pinned edition are distinct and may coexist. Do not invent VCN withdrawal or predicate equivalence. Its containing finite DAG with a retained declaration always has at least one tip.

Historical evidence citations require membership closure, never continued current activity. A noncurrent typed pin blocks present eligibility even if its metadata-only successor appears equivalent. Renew the selected typed chain explicitly; the original historical designation remains inspectable.

| Case | Exact outer conclusion / required behavior |
| --- | --- |
| No FC positive record for S, including unknown but well-formed endpoint IDs | `not_declared`; no governing group; reason `fc_not_declared` |
| FC records exist but every FC root is inactive | `inactive`; historical groups retained; reason `fc_inactive` |
| Any active FC same-root fork | `conflicted`; no governing group; include `fc_same_root_conflict`, plus cross-root disagreement if independently present |
| No FC fork but active independent FC bodies differ | `disagreed`; no governing group; `fc_cross_root_disagreement` |
| Healthy agreeing FC roots, lower eligibility reasons empty | `applicable`; retain governing group index; empty reasons |
| Healthy agreeing FC roots, any lower eligibility reason | `inapplicable`; retain governing group index and ordered reasons; never predicate falsity |
| Inactive historical FC disagreement beside healthy agreement | Inactive roots disclosed; otherwise same applicable/inapplicable algorithm |
| Equal-body healthy independent FM roots | Corroborate; no lower reason solely for multiplicity |
| Equal-body same-root FM fork | `inapplicable` when FC agrees; `fm_same_root_conflict` |
| Active different-body FM roots | `inapplicable`; `fm_cross_root_disagreement` |
| Witness withdrawn, another healthy identical B remains | May be `applicable`; witness retained and disclosed, never substituted |
| Every active assertion of B lost | `inapplicable`; `fm_body_not_current`, also `fm_inactive` if no active FM root remains |
| Exact pinned VCN stale | `inapplicable`; `vcn_not_current` |
| VCN conflicted | `inapplicable`; `vcn_conflicted`, plus `vcn_not_current` if the pin has a successor |
| Stale/conflicted/inactive/unaligned typed FLD/VPA/VSM or PCN/PIF | `inapplicable`; typed-support reasons and exact offending rows |
| Missing witness/support/model evidence, wrong target or invalid closure | Typed structural failure before any conclusion; not a valid inapplicable world |

No observation, zero/null/sign/unit difference, source disagreement or acquisition-cohort change affects FC. A present designation need not be numerically evaluable; CN can still return `not_evaluated`. FC conclusion `applicable` is not a system state.
