## 1. Decision, authority and verified baseline

Recommend one separately authorized, application-owned synthetic proof with two
outputs that never substitute for one another:

1. **Dependency review:** for complete before/proposed worlds and an exact named
   inventory, identify which decisions require reevaluation and through which
   admitted semantic dependency.

   assess a supplied target, action feasibility and finite-set cost selection.

## 3. Exact synthetic model

### 3.1 Variables, units, equation and step order

The hypothetical enclosure contains body `B`, heater `H`, fan `F`, sensor `S` and
controller `C`. Declared ports/connections are topology only. They supply no effect.

| Symbol | Meaning and unit |
| --- | --- |
| `T0`, `T1` | body temperature at step boundaries, `degC` |
| `A` | ambient temperature during the step, `degC` |
| `u_h`, `u_f` | hypothetical issued controller-command states, dimensionless `0/1` |
| `h`, `f` | actual physical operating states used by the law, dimensionless `0/1` |
| `alpha`, `gamma` | heater/fan temperature contribution, `degC/step` |
| `beta` | fractional relaxation coefficient, `1/step` |

The one-step law is

`T1 = T0 + alpha*h - gamma*f - beta*(T0-A)`.

Baseline exact values are `T0=40 degC`, `A=20 degC`, `u_h=0`, `u_f=1`,
`alpha=10 degC/step`, `gamma=4 degC/step`, and `beta=1/5 per step`.
All numeric values are finite exact rationals. All intervals are closed. No
clipping, saturation, rounding, probability or confidence meaning applies.

The sole evaluation order is:

1. validate the complete world and declared applicability;
2. map a nominated hypothesis to actual pre-intervention `h,f`;
3. evaluate action preconditions and the exact referenced effect edition;
4. if projection is admitted, apply its effect once at step start;
5. hold declared invariants for the step and evaluate the law once;
6. retain the raw exact set;
7. separately compare that set with the target;
8. separately assess each action across retained hypotheses; and
9. separately minimize cost over the finite supplied feasible set.

No dependency edge enters this sequence as an effect or coefficient.

### 3.2 Nominated hypotheses and actions

The hypotheses are synthetic nominated alternatives, not an exhaustive causal set:

- `H_H`: before intervention, heater is stuck on, so `h=1`; fan follows `f=u_f`.
- `H_F`: before intervention, fan is failed off, so `f=0`; heater follows `h=u_h`.

The finite action set is supplied, not generated:

| Action | Preconditions | Explicit effect source | Step invariants | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| `a0` | model applicable | `E-a0@1`: identity | all inputs/law unchanged | 0 |
| `aH` | isolation capability true; synthetic permission true; before-step timing valid | `E-aH@1`: force `h:=0` before the law | `T0,A,f,beta,gamma` and law form unchanged | 2 |
| `aF` | restoration capability true; synthetic permission true; before-step timing valid | `E-aF@1`: force `f:=1` before the law | `T0,A,h,alpha,beta` and law form unchanged | 3 |
| `aHF` | both component preconditions; nonconflicting effects | canonical composition of `E-aH@1`, then `E-aF@1`; distinct targets make order result-equivalent | `T0,A,beta` and law form unchanged | 5 |

Effect IDs and editions are application-local premise identities, not registered
backend records. The complete objects and their external content commitments provide
proof-local identity and provenance only.

### 3.3 Commands, assumed interventions, execution and observations

These meanings are disjoint:

- `u_h,u_f` are supplied hypothetical command-state premises. A command does not
  establish actual `h,f`, an intervention effect or physical success.
- Nominating an action means including it in the finite comparison set. It is not an
  issued command or authorization.
- Projecting an action means counterfactually applying its **assumed** effect rule
  after its synthetic gates pass. It is a calculation, not execution evidence.
- Successful physical execution would require separate authenticated execution
  evidence and domain semantics. No such fact or input is accepted by this proof.
- An observation remains an observation with source/time provenance. Even a later
  value equal to a projection proves neither issuance, success nor cause.
- `T1` emitted here is calculated. It is never persisted or passed to an
  observed-state evaluator as a `TemporalOccurrence`.

No equipment is operated and no physical claim is made.

## 4. Applicability, uncertainty and failure semantics

### 4.1 Applicability and premise support

Baseline applicability is a complete synthetic assertion for exactly one step,
constant `A`, the named configuration and law edition, before-step effects, finite
inputs and no unmodeled action. Each required precondition, timing rule, effect
edition and invariance assertion has three-valued support: `true`, `false` or
`unknown/missing`, with provenance.

- explicit false action precondition makes that action `inapplicable`;
- unknown/missing precondition, timing or required invariance makes affected raw
  predictions `unknown`;
- explicit false global model applicability makes all raw/target/feasibility rows
  `inapplicable` and selection `inapplicable_for_declared_model`;
- unknown global applicability makes all those decisions and selection `unknown`;
- a missing effect makes actions that require it `unknown`;
- an internally inconsistent or stale exact effect reference refuses the entire
  projection request as an integrity-invalid bundle; it is not scientific unknown.

No missing value inherits a baseline value. No topology, command name or prior result
repairs unavailable support.

### 4.2 Set projection and target assessment

A complete numeric support is a closed exact rational set. For interval premises,
the projector evaluates the affine law over the declared joint domain by exact corner
enumeration. A shared parameter is one joint coordinate and is not independently
resampled between terms. Any required correlation or constraint must be carried;
missing joint semantics yields `unknown`.

For ready raw set `Y` and closed target `G`:

- `guaranteed` iff `Y` is a subset of `G`, including equal endpoints;
- `possible_not_guaranteed` iff `Y` intersects `G` but is not a subset;
- `incompatible` iff `Y` and `G` are disjoint; and
- `unknown` if raw support or target support is incomplete.

`incompatible` is conditional exclusion under supplied complete premises. It is not
empirical falsification or proof of an alternative.

### 4.3 Action feasibility and finite selection

For each nominated action, feasibility is assessed only over retained hypothesis set
`{H_H}`:

- `guaranteed` iff every retained-hypothesis target decision is guaranteed;
- `possible_not_guaranteed` iff support is complete, no retained row is incompatible,
  and at least one is possible but not guaranteed;
- `incompatible` if complete support makes at least one retained row incompatible;
- `inapplicable` if the action has an explicitly false precondition; and
- `unknown` if any relevant premise or retained row is unknown.

Selection is evaluated only when every nominated action needed to compare minima has
a complete feasibility disposition. It returns:

- `selected_from_supplied_set` plus every canonically ordered equal-cost action among
  guaranteed actions;
- `no_feasible_action_in_supplied_set` when complete evidence shows every supplied
  admissible action is not guaranteed;
- `unknown` when incomplete information could conceal feasibility or change the
  minimum;
- `inapplicable_for_declared_model` when the model's global applicability is false;
  or
- `refused` when the bundle or policy is invalid.

Costs are nonnegative exact scalars and never enter the physical equation. The claim
is minimum only over four supplied actions, one retained hypothesis set, the supplied
target and this policy. “No feasible action in the supplied set” never means “no plan
exists,” and no action is generated or authorized.

## 8. Complete request and result forms

These are experiment-local forms, not a public API proposal.

### 8.1 Dependency review

The request has exactly: `version`, `design_sha256`, `operation`, `request_id`,
`before_public_material`, `proposed_public_material`, `before_application_world`,
`proposed_application_world`, `decision_inventory`, `field_map`, `scope`,

A successful response has envelope `operation_status="complete"`, one canonically

outcome. Invalid material, commitments, inventory or field map yields
`operation_status="refused"`, `result=null` and one exact ordered diagnostic set.

### 8.2 Projection, target, feasibility and selection

The request has exactly: `version`, `design_sha256`, `operation`, `request_id`,
`public_material`, one complete `application_world`, `hypothesis_ids`, `action_ids`,
`retained_hypothesis_ids`, `decision_inventory_commitment`,
`externally_expected_commitments`, and `limits`. It receives no dependency-review
answer, expected result, case ID, oracle digest as an answer selector or precomputed
matrix.

A valid mixed response has `operation_status="complete"` and four separate result
collections:

1. raw rows with decision ID; row status `ready|unknown|inapplicable`; hypothesis
   mapping; preconditions; effect source/edition; timing; post-effect input domain;
   exact operands/corners; raw interval; completeness; stop reason; and provenance;
2. target rows with decision ID; raw decision reference; status; raw interval;
   target interval; `guaranteed|possible_not_guaranteed|incompatible|null`; exact
   endpoint trace; and provenance;
3. feasibility rows with decision ID; retained hypotheses; contributing target rows;
   `guaranteed|possible_not_guaranteed|incompatible|unknown|inapplicable`; and trace;
4. selection with decision ID; considered/admissible/unknown/inapplicable actions;
   per-action feasibility; costs; status; minimum cost or null; all tied selections;
   finite-scope statement; and provenance.

Whole-request `refused` has `result=null` and no partial rows. Thus C4 and C9 can
carry ready and unknown/inapplicable rows without confusing partial semantic status
with operation failure.

### 8.3 Diagnostics and refusal preparation

Preparation must freeze exact codes, JSON-pointer targets and order under this
precedence:

1. incrementally enforced resource limit before unsafe construction;
2. wire/schema/type/numeric-form invalidity;
3. external or internal commitment mismatch;
4. malformed or invalid public material/result;
5. before/proposed world or claimed-delta inconsistency;
6. inventory/field-map incompleteness or unsupported role; and
7. premise reference/edition conflict, including M2's stale exact effect reference.

Missing scientific support, false/unknown applicability and false/unknown
preconditions are typed semantic results, not schema refusals. Preparation must add
only structural/refusal/resource twins necessary to prove each precedence boundary,
with a nearest valid twin and complete success/refusal output. An unsupported claim
of physical execution is refused; observations can be carried only under their
separate non-causal role.

Every response carries `input_sha256`, design/package commitments, exact public
revision/result commitments, both relevant world commitments, premise-manifest
commitment, calculation classification and oracle-access disclosure. Application
premise provenance never upgrades those objects into backend records or physical
truth.

## 11. Independent oracle and falsifiability

serialization primitives:
