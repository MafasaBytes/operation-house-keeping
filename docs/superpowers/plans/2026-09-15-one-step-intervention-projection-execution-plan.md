## 3. Oracle and runtime separation

The package retains three expected-reasoning implementations:

1. complete dual-world/public-material comparison plus a hand-authored 22-decision
   field map;
2. exact `Fraction`-based hypothesis/effect/law evaluation with endpoint corners; and
3. independent closed-set target tests, retained-hypothesis feasibility and exhaustive
   finite cost selection with plural ties.

They share only canonical JSON, reference resolution and rational parsing. They share
the owner-approved equation, effects, hard-support and policy assumptions; they do
not supply empirical independence. The same preparing assistant authored them, and
that provenance remains explicit. No future runtime implementation exists in or was
called by this rehearsal. Before execution, runtime source must be written only after
the retained oracle commitment is verified, then frozen and shown not to import or
read oracle/expected-result objects.

The runtime may receive only a role's complete resolved request, approved limits and
independent trust pins. It must not receive the case ID as a dispatch selector,
expected output, oracle object, expected digest as an answer selector or precomputed
matrix. Parent-side comparison may access expected objects only after the runtime
emits its complete result.
